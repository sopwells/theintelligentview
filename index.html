Metric 01
Revenue to Tech Spend
Measures how much revenue is generated relative to total tech investment. Higher = greater top-line productivity.

Tech Capex Intensity
The proportion of total tech spend allocated to capital investment. Higher = company likely in active build-out mode.
Metric 03
Tech Efficiency %
Assesses how much of a company's earnings are consumed by its tech costs. Lower = more efficient tech estate.
Metric 04
Tech Opex
The operational component of total tech spend — reflects the ongoing cost of running and maintaining a company's technology.
